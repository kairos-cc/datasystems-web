Questions

How is it delivered?

You enter your GitHub username at checkout. After payment, an invitation to the private repository is sent to that account automatically. Accept it from your GitHub notifications or email, then clone the repository.

What do I need before I start?

A Databricks workspace with Unity Catalog on AWS or Azure, Terraform 1.5 or later, Databricks CLI 0.240 or later, and the AWS CLI v2 or Azure CLI 2.60 or later. Creating the storage credential, external locations and catalogs needs the matching Unity Catalog privileges on your metastore.

Who can use the Small Team license?

Everyone in the purchasing team or organization, for internal use, including single-tenant client environments you manage directly. You can't publish, resell or repackage the code. See the EULA.

What's your refund policy?

You can request a refund within 14 days of purchase. If a refund is issued, your license ends, repository access is revoked and you must delete your copies. See the Refund Policy.

How do I get help?

Email info@kairoscc.io. The README also includes runbooks for state lock deadlocks, Azure role propagation and CLI version issues.
