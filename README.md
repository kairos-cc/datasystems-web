## Rules: keep Paddle approval
- **This domain sells digital products only.** Paddle rejected kairoscc.io (Nov 23, 2026) because it advertised IT, consulting and software development services, which its Acceptable Use Policy doesn't support. Don't add consulting, services, custom development, or other products (Rackside) here, and don't link to the company site.
- The legal entity name, Kairos Consulting Collective, LLC, stays in the footer and legal pages as the seller.
- **Checkout:** the live client token, `priceId` and `customData.github_username` must not change. Make.com reads `github_username` from Paddle's webhook to send the GitHub invite.
